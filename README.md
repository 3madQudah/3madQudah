### 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm a **Data Science graduate from the University of Jordan** who builds AI systems end to end, from the data and the model to the API, the UI, and the deployment. I care about systems that are measured, not just demoed: every project ships with an evaluation layer and real numbers.

- 🧠 **RAG in production**: hybrid retrieval (BM25 + FAISS), metadata filtering, source citations, and safe text-to-SQL over live databases
- 🤖 **Agentic AI**: deterministic **LangGraph** workflows with grounding checks, confidence routing, and human-in-the-loop review
- 🗣️ **Arabic-first AI**: Arabic, English, and code-switched speech and text, evaluated per language
- 📊 **Applied ML**: forecasting, churn prediction, and classification turned into interactive dashboards
- 🏢 Previously: **Data Science Intern at Zain Jordan** (customer churn and mall footfall analytics)
- 📍 Amman, Jordan · open to AI / ML Engineer roles
